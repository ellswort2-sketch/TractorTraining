Transmission bench · PowrReverser 16F/16R

Select a range, gear, and travel direction. This is a stationary control demonstration: no vehicle movement or actual ground speed is calculated.

A1 · NEUTRAL · stationary teaching model

Four gears × four ranges = 16 combinations in each direction. PowrReverser uses an electrohydraulic directional reverser; its gear/range arrangement is not a hydrostatic drive or a CVT.

For this manual configuration, a range change requires the clutch and a stopped tractor; speed-gear shifts use the clutch. The PowrReverser direction lever can change direction without depressing the clutch. That capability does not authorize abrupt reversal at speed. Follow the actual tractor’s operating procedures.

Try two gear/range combinations, then select Neutral.

Hydraulic circuit bench

A simplified double-acting cylinder illustrates a rear remote circuit. Select a function and watch which side receives oil. The drawing illustrates flow, not the full tractor plumbing or hitch circuit.

Neutral: the cylinder is stationary. The simplified open-center supply path returns pump flow to the reservoir.

Orange = pressure supply; blue = return. Neutral holding assumes a serviceable simplified valve/cylinder; do not rely on hydraulics alone to support a raised load. Flow animation is illustrative and does not estimate pressure, flow rate, or cycle time.

Connect the function to the hardware

The SCV lever directs flow to its matching pair of rear couplers. Connected hoses carry oil to opposite ends of a double-acting cylinder.

The three-point hitch has its own position control. The PTO transmits rotary mechanical power; it is not a hydraulic hose connection.

Before touching a real hydraulic connection

  • Lower or securely support equipment as directed by the manual.
  • Stop the engine and relieve pressure using the prescribed procedure.
  • Never search for a pinhole leak with your hand.
  • A suspected fluid-injection injury needs immediate emergency medical attention, even when the puncture appears small.

Do not hold a remote lever against the end of cylinder travel. That can heat the oil and damage components.
