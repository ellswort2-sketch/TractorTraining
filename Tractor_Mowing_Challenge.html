Mission briefing

Use the rear PTO mower to cut as much of the playing area as possible in five minutes. Aim for at least 70% coverage, minimal overlap, and clean turns. A wide mower is used so this is achievable within the time limit.

Scored operating target: 5–9 km/h and 1,900–2,200 engine RPM. Start with B3 at 2,200 RPM (about 7 km/h). Engage the PTO at low RPM while stopped, then raise RPM. Lower the mower for cutting; raise it for turns and disengage the PTO before reversing.

These are fixed game targets for fair comparison, not specifications or operating instructions for a particular mower. This teaching model uses immediate braking and simplified steering; it does not simulate real PTO ratios, slopes, obstacles, draft, acceleration, or equipment safety systems.

How your score is calculated
  • Coverage: 40 × min(field fraction cut ÷ 0.70, 1).
  • Pass accuracy: up to 25 points, reduced by repeat-cut area ÷ all cut area across passes. No cutting earns zero accuracy points.
  • Operating technique: 20 × fraction of moving time spent cutting forward at target speed and RPM with the mower lowered and PTO on.
  • Control procedure: up to 15 points, minus 3 per recorded error; no movement earns zero.

Errors: attempting a gear/range shift without clutch; changing direction while moving; reversing with PTO engaged; engaging PTO above 1,200 RPM or while moving. Events are logged, and invalid selections are blocked.

Accuracy, technique, and procedure points are multiplied by min(coverage ÷ 25%, 1), so mowing a tiny strip and waiting does not earn most of the points. Only a completed five-minute challenge is eligible for class comparison. A challenge cannot be paused; switching tabs stops tractor motion while the countdown continues. Practice and early finishes are labeled in the report. Reports are browser-generated formative records and can be edited; they are not verified competition records.
